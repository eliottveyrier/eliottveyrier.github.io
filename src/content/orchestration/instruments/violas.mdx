> Let us observe some differences between the viola and the violin, which should help define its function in the orchestra. 
> 1. The viola bow is slightly heavier than that for the violin.
> 2. The strings are thicker and more resistant to the bow, which necessitates a firmer stroke to produce a full sound. Lighter bowings are also possible but are more difficult to produce. 
> 3. **Harmonics** are easier to play because the thicker strings produce them more reliably. 

-- Samuel Adler, *The Study of Orchestration*
