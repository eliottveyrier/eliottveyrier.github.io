## Main Characteristics

The violin family has many advantages and was the first to be fully developed.
Namely :

- a gigantic range covering 7 octaves from <NoteSpan note="E1"/> or <NoteSpan note="C1"/> to <NoteSpan note="E7"/>
- its homogeneous tone color throughout its entire range
- wide dynamic range
- a richness of tone quality and expressivity
- versatility
- its ability to sound continuously, without breathing

### Usual section sizes

first violins : 8 or 9 stands of two players each (16 to 18 players)

second violins : 7 or 8 stands of two players each (14 to 16 players)

violas : 5 or 6 stands of two players each (10 to 12 players)

cellos : 5 or 6 stands of two players each (10 to 12 players)

double basses : 4 or 5 stands of two players each (8 to 10 players)

## Divided Strings

Two lines at the same time are often marked *divisi*, implying the *outside* player (sitting right-side of the stand)
plays the top note, with the *inside* player plays the lower notes.

no *divisi* marking implies that the part is played with double stops.
*non-div* can ensure there is no ambiguity in that case.
